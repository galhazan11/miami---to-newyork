Exciting Start

Beach & Fun
Miami → West Palm Beach
Morning: Swimming in warm tropical waters 🌊
Afternoon: Visit wildlife park - animals in nature 🦜
Evening: Sunset cocktails - paradise in a glass 🍹
Night: Dinner at local beachfront restaurant 🌙
3
Historic Charm
West Palm → St. Augustine
Morning: Drive 250 km north 🚗
Afternoon: Tour oldest city - St. Augustine 🏰
Evening: Walk through tropical gardens 🌴
Night: Dinner in historic colonial house 🌟
4
Coastal Beauty
St. Augustine → Charleston
Morning: Visit historic churches - heritage sites 🙏
Afternoon: River cruise - natural wetlands 🛥️
Evening: Tour Rainbow Row - colorful historic district 🎨
Night: Wine tasting at art gallery 🍷
5
Southern Charm
Charleston → Savannah
Morning: Walk Forsyth Park - nature in city center 🌳
Afternoon: Photography at scenic viewpoints 📸
Evening: Shopping at antique shops & galleries 🛍️
Night: Live jazz bar - authentic Southern vibes 🎷
6
Beach Paradise
Savannah → Myrtle Beach
Morning: Beach swimming & water sports 🏄
Afternoon: Amusement park & boardwalk fun 🎡
Evening: Ice cream & sunset beach walk 🍦
Night: Bonfire on the beach 🔥
7
Capital Discovery
Myrtle Beach → Washington DC
Morning: Tour the Capitol - American icon 🏛️
Afternoon: Smithsonian Museum - American history 🎭
Evening: Night photography at Lincoln Memorial ✨
Night: Dinner in Georgetown - acclaimed restaurant 🍽️
8
Liberty Bell
DC → Philadelphia
Morning: Visit Liberty Bell - American independence 🔔
Afternoon: Walk Independence Hall - historic path 📜
Evening: Art gallery visit in historic district 🎨
Night: Famous Philly cheesesteak dinner 🥪
9
Big Apple Arrival
Philadelphia → New York City
Morning: Welcome to NYC! Times Square 🌆
Afternoon: Broadway tour - world theater capital 🎭
Evening: Shopping on 5th Avenue - fashion capital 👗
Night: Rooftop champagne with Manhattan views 🥂
10
Final Celebration!
New York City 🗽
Morning: Statue of Liberty - symbol of freedom 🗽
Afternoon: Empire State Building - iconic views 🏙️
Evening: Central Park stroll - green heart of NYC 🌳
Night: Grand celebration - memories for a lifetime! 🎉
